The company

Altisource describes its mission as "helping servicers, originators and investors maximize results, minimize costs and mitigate risks." Its businesses span mortgage originations, servicing, and a suite of integrated services for single-family rental investors to buy and sell real estate, which is where the buy-box and underwriting platform I led fit.

RentRange

RentRange is the rental data business I joined Altisource through. It describes itself as "a trusted provider in the real estate investment space for over a decade," selling property reports, market metric reports, investor lists and property valuation models. The Rental Automated Valuation Model I led was built on RentRange data.
