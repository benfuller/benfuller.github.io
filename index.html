About

I've been the first engineer in the door, the co-founder who set the architecture and the hiring bar, and the leader directing 50+ engineers across four business units through their managers. I've built platforms from the first commit and grown engineering teams from one person to fifteen, reaching thousands of customers as a co-founder.

These days my work is AI-native: multi-agent systems, RAG, tool and function calling, and MCP integrations, with the evaluation, guardrails, and governance that keep AI a force multiplier instead of a liability. I care about outcomes over activity, I stay close to the code, and I build the kind of engineering culture where strong people do their best work.

15+ years spanning early-stage builds and NASDAQ-scale organizations. I think like a product owner and stay technical enough to challenge a model, interrogate an incident, and keep the trust of the engineers I lead.

I've shipped under real compliance bars throughout my career: SOC 2, HIPAA/CLIA-accredited healthcare infrastructure with zero audit findings, and regulated financial and mortgage data at a NASDAQ company.

As a US citizen based in Barcelona with full EU work authorization, I can join teams on either side of the Atlantic with no sponsorship required.
